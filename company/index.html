Company

Sadeem Robotics

A physical AI company from Oman that designs and deploys service robots for front-of-house work: greeting visitors, guiding them, and handing them to the right person. Our robot is designed in Oman, its body is made in Oman, and it speaks Omani Arabic.

People

Al Mualla Al Maawali

Co-founder, CEO
LI

Leads the company, the product and every customer deployment. Designed the robot's body and leads how it comes together, from first sketch to a working unit on a customer's floor. Before Sadeem, co-developed the image-classification stage of SpaceBorne AI, an edge AI system that runs on board a satellite now in orbit. It's the same problem a robot has: AI that makes its decisions on the device itself, on limited hardware, with no cloud to lean on.

B.Eng. Electrical & Computer Engineering, Sultan Qaboos University

Abdulmunim Al Busaidi

LI

B.Sc. Electrical & Computer Engineering, Sultan Qaboos University
