What it does

Four things,
done well.

SADEEM isn't trying to be everything. It's built around a small set of jobs that matter to operators every day — and it does each one without supervision.

01 / Endurance

Runs all day.

A full shift on a single charge. Returns to its dock automatically when it needs power. No babysitting.

02 / Voice

Speaks naturally.

Understands plain Arabic and English. Tell it what you need — it listens, replies and gets it done.

03 / Payload

Carries the load.

Up to 20 kilograms. Meals, supplies, parcels, documents — anything that needs to move from A to B.

04 / Navigation

Knows its way.

Maps your space, navigates around people and obstacles. No tracks, no markers, no remote control needed.

A 01

HD touchscreen

10.1-inch display with expressive face. Touch to interact, glance to see status.

A 02

Far-field microphones

Hears you from across the lobby. Filters background noise on the fly.

A 03

Ambient light strip

Soft amber band signals status — patrolling, paused, low battery.

B 01

Cargo bay

Single 20 kg compartment with a lockable lid. Configurable for trays, parcels, linens.

B 02

Multi-sensor LiDAR

360° obstacle detection. Sees stairs, glass walls and the cat under the chair.

B 03

Auto-dock base

Returns to charge on its own. Four hours to a full battery.

From box to busy floor

Live in a day.

No construction. No tracks taped to the floor. No special hardware to install. SADEEM learns your space the way a new hire would — by walking through it once.

01

Unbox.

Wheel it out. Plug in the dock. Power it on. The robot wakes up and finds your Wi-Fi.

02

Walk & map.

Drive it once around the floor with the tablet. It builds a map of every corridor and room as it goes.

03

Mark & name.

Drop pins on rooms, exits, the kitchen, the dock. Tag them in your language. Done in twenty minutes.

04

Set it loose.

Press start. SADEEM goes to work — and gets better at your floor every day.
